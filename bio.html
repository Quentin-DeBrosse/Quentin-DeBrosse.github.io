Biography

Quentin DeBrosse is a composer, conductor, and trombonist dedicated to creating meaningful musical experiences through storytelling and collaboration.

Beginning music at a young age, Quentin developed a passion for performance and composition, writing works for wind ensemble, orchestra, and other ensembles.

His music focuses on atmosphere, emotion, and creating immersive experiences for both performers and listeners.
