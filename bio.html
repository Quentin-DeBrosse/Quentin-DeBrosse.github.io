Biography

Quentin DeBrosse is a composer, conductor, and trombonist dedicated to creating meaningful musical experiences through storytelling and collaboration. Beginning music at a young age, Quentin has developed a passion for composition, performance, and exploring the connection between musicians and audiences.

His works for wind ensemble and other ensembles focus on atmosphere, emotion, and the ability of music to transport listeners into new worlds.

Compositional Philosophy

Music is more than sound — it is storytelling. Every piece should create a world for performers and audiences to enter, leaving behind an experience that lasts beyond the final note.
